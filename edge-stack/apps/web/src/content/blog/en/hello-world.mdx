### Why built for the Edge?

Edge computing moves logic closer to your users. By using **Cloudflare Workers** and **Neon DB**, we provide a global-ready infrastructure out of the box.

- **Zero Cold Starts**: V8 isolates are fast.
- **Strictly Typed**: End-to-end safety.
- **Global Latency**: Speed from anywhere.
